# Philadelphia Fed Index

[Philadelphia Fed Index](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/united-states/philadelphia-fed-business-conditions) is based on the results of survey questioning manufacturers in Philadelphia on their attitude towards the current economic situation. The figures below "0" are an indication of a slowing economy.

This index has a limited impact on the market. This index is being carefully watched, because it is published prior to [ISM Index](ISM-Index.md) and can give an idea of what it will be. Growth of the index leads to the growth of the dollar.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 10:00 EST, the third Thursday of the month.
  * Source: Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia.
