# Chicago PMI Index

[Chicago PMI Index](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/united-states/chicago-pmi) is based on surveys of purchasing managers in Chicago. This index includes the status of production orders, the prices of manufactured products and inventories in warehouses. Figures below the 45-50 indicate a slowing economy.

The indicator is carefully watched, because it is published shortly before the release of [ISM Index](ISM-Index.md). It has a significant impact on the market because it can give an idea of what the [ISM Index](ISM-Index.md) will be. Growth of the index leads to the growth of the dollar.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 10:00 EST, the last business day of the month.
  * Source: the Purchasing Managers Association of Chicago.
