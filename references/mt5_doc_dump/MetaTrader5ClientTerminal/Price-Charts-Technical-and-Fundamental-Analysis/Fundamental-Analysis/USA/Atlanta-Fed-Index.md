# Atlanta Fed Index/Index of manufacturing in states under jurisdiction of the Atlanta Federal Reserve

This index represents the results of a survey of manufacturers in Atlanta for their attitudes towards the current economic situation. The figures below "0" are an indication of a slowing economy. The index has a limited impact on the market, because it is published after the release of an indicator of business activity at the national level ([ISM index](ISM-Index.md)). The growing figures of this index is a favorable factor for the growth of the dollar.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 09:00 EST, after the 10th of the month.
  * Source: Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta.
