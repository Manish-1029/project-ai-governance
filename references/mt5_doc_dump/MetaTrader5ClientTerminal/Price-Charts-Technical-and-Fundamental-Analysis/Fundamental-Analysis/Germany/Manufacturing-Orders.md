# Manufacturing Orders

The indicator shows the change in the number of orders for products manufactured by German companies. It reflects the country's economic prospects. The indicator is taken into account by the market.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 11:00 GMT, 4-7th of the month following the reporting period.
