# Manufacturing Activity

A report released by Business New Zealand commenting on the PMI, the New Zealand manufacturing sector, and noteworthy trends. Increased activity in the manufacturing sector is usually a precursor to economic expansion and inflationary pressures.

This index summarizes key points regarding the data gathered by the monthly business surveys sent to manufacturers and gives a concise breakdown of how optimistic manufacturers feel in the short-term.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 22:45 GMT, two weeks after the reporting period.
  * Source: Business New Zealand.
