# Labour Cost Index

The indicator reflects the quarterly change in wages and other income received by working population. Rising values have a positive effect on the national currency, because when companies pay more for labor they are likely to transfer their costs to the end consumer, which in turn affects the [Consumer Price Index](Consumer-Price-Index.md).

  * Release Frequency: quarterly.
  * Release Schedule: 22:45 GMT.
  * Source: Statistics New Zealand.
