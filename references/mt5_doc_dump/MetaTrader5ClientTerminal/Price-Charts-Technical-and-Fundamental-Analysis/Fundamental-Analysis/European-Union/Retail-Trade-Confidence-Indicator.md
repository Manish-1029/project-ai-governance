# Retail Trade Confidence Indicator

This index is calculated similarly to [Consumer Confidence Index](Consumer-Confidence-Indicator.md). Owners of retail businesses answer questions about the trading situation at the moment and estimate prospects for the future.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 09:00 GMT, the last week of the month.
  * Source: European Commission, Directorate General for Economic and Financial Affairs.
