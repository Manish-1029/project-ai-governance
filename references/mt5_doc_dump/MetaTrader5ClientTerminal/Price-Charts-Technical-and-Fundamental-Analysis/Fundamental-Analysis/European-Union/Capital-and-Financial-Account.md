# Capital and Financial Account

This indicator is a part of [Balance of Payments](Balance-of-Payments.md). The Capital and Financial Account is the ratio of the movement of public and private funds into and out of a country, received and issued credits and results of transactions on government reserves.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 9:00 GMT, about 50 days after the reporting month.
  * Source: Eurostat.
