# Industrial Confidence Indicator

The [Industrial Confidence index](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/european-union/industrial-confidence-indicator) is calculated similarly to [Consumer Confidence Index](Consumer-Confidence-Indicator.md). Management of industrial enterprises evaluates the production prospects in general, as well as the prospects of orders growth and industrial stocks growth.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 09:00 GMT, the last week of the month.
  * Source: European Commission, Directorate General for Economic and Financial Affairs.
