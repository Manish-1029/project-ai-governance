# Retail Sales

This is the [index of change in retail sales](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/switzerland/retail-sales-yy). This is an important indicator of consumer spendings, and is used for calculating the [Consumer Price Index](Consumer-Price-Index.md). Is measured as a percentage.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 07:15 GMT.
  * Source: Swiss Federal Statistical Office.
