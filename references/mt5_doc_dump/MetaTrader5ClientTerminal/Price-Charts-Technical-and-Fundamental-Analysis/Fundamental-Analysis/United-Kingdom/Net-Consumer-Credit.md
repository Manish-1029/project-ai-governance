# Net Consumer Credit

The [amount of loans](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/united-kingdom/boe-net-lending-to-individuals) granted to individuals over the last month. A large value of the indicator can talk about "overheating" of the economy, when consumers take more credits than it is necessary for normal living. A too high level of credits can lead to a recession in the long term if the consumer is too burdened by the loan and will have to reduce consumption or to leave the debt to a financier in case of bankruptcy.

It has a limited impact on the market.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Source: The Bank of England.
