# Industrial Production

It is a major indicator of economic health: the production is the main driving force behind the country's economic system and reacts quickly to the ups and downs of the business cycle.

This indicator calculates the change in the total value of products produced in mining and manufacturing, as well as services taking into account inflation.

  * Release Frequency: quarterly.
  * Release Schedule: 02:00 GMT.
  * Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China.
