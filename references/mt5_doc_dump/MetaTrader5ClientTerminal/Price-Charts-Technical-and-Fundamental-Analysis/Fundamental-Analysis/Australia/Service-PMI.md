# Service PMI

The [Service PMI](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/australia/aig-services-index) indicator is determined based on monthly surveys of executives in the services sector. The purpose of the survey is to assess changes in the industry.

Index reading below 50 basis points indicate that services activity is generally declining. The formation of the final indicator is influenced not only by objective factors but also by psychological ones (subjective assessment of respondents). This adds weight to Service PMI, and therefore bidders pay attention to this indicator.
