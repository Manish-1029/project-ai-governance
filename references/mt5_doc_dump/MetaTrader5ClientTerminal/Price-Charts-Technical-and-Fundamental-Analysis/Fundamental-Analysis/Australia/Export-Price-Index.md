# Export Price Index

This is the indicator that reflects changes in the price of exported goods and services. The [Export Price index](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/australia/export-price-index-qq) is one of indicators of inflationary pressures. With expectations of an increase in basic interest rates, the increase of the index leads to an increase in the rate of Australian dollar.

  * Release Frequency: quarterly.
  * Source: Australian Bureau of Statistics.
