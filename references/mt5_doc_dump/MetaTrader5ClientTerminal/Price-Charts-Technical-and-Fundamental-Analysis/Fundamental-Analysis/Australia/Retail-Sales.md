# Retail Sales

Measure of the total [monthly sales of goods and services by retail stores](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/australia/retail-sales-mm) in Australia. Retail Sales is an important measure of consumer spending and inflationary pressures in Australia. Steady increases in retail sales apply significant inflationary pressures to consumer prices.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 22:45 GMT, monthly, roughly 30 days following the end of the reporting month.
  * Source: Australian Bureau of Statistics.
