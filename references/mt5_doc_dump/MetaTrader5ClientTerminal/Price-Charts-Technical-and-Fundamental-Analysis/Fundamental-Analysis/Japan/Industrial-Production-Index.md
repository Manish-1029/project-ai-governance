# Industrial Production Index

The [index of industrial production](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/japan/industrial-production-mm). It shows changes in industrial production in the country. The growth of this index leads to growth of the national currency. It has a significant impact on the market.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 23:50 GMT, preliminary index is released at the end of each month, in two weeks revised figures come out. 
  * Source: Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry.
