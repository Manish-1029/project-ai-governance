# Machinery Orders

The [Machinery Orders](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/japan/core-machinery-orders-mm) indicator reflects the level of business capital spending and business activity. It is calculated based on evaluations of more than 300 industrial manufacturers. It has a significant impact on the market.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 05:00 GMT, in the middle of the second month following the reporting period.
  * Source: Economic and Social Research Institute.
