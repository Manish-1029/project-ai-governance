# Tertiary Industry Index

The index shows the level of [activity in the service sector](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/japan/tertiary-industry-activity-index-mm) taking into account all areas of this segment of the economy. It is a leading indicator taken into account by bidders, predicting the future dynamics of the non-production index [Tankan](Tankan-Survey.md).

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 23:50 GMT, 2nd-3rd week after the reporting period.
  * Source: Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry.
