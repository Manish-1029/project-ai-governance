# Trade Balance

[Trade Balance](https://www.mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/japan/trade-balance) is the difference between the total value of exports and the total value of imports. A positive trade balance shows the demand of goods of the country on the international market, as well as the fact that the country does not consume all that it produces. A negative trade balance suggests that the country consumes foreign goods together with it own goods.

Because Japan is an export-oriented country, this information gives a critical insight into the development of the country's economy and changes in foreign exchange rates.

A positive trade balance acts as an appreciating weight on the yen.

  * Release Frequency: monthly.
  * Release Schedule: 23:50 GMT, the second week after the reporting period.
  * Source: Ministry of Finance, Customs Office
